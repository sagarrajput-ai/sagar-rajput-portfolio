Subnet mask vs. wildcard mask

A subnet mask identifies which portion of an IPv4 address represents the network and which portion represents hosts.

A wildcard mask is the inverse of the subnet mask. It is commonly used in Cisco IOS configurations, access-control lists and OSPF network statements.

Subnet & Wildcard Mask FAQ

What is a wildcard mask?

A wildcard mask is the inverse of the subnet mask. It is commonly used in Cisco IOS access-control lists and OSPF network statements.

What is the difference between a subnet mask and a wildcard mask?

A subnet mask identifies the network and host portions of an address by marking network bits as 1. A wildcard mask inverts this, marking the bits Cisco should ignore when matching addresses.

What is CIDR notation?

A way of writing an IP network as an address followed by a slash and the number of bits used for the network portion, such as /24.
