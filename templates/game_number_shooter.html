HOW TO PLAY

How to play Number Shooter

  1. Aim.

    Move your mouse, or drag your finger, across the columns. The highlighted lane shows where your tile will go.

  2. Shoot.

    Click or release to fire the tile up the lane. It sticks under the last tile in that column.

  3. Merge matching numbers.

    Tiles that touch a matching number merge into a bigger one. Merging three or more at once makes an even bigger tile, and merges can chain.

  4. Don't fill a column.

    If a column fills all the way down to the shooter, the game is over.
