How to play Packet Runner

  1. Jump firewalls.

    Press Space, Up, or tap Jump to clear orange firewall walls on the ground.

  2. Duck packet loss.

    Press Down or hold Duck to drop under floating red packet-loss obstacles.

  3. Survive as long as possible.

    Speed increases the longer you run. Your score is how far your packet travels.
