HOW TO PLAY

How to play Memory Match

  1. Flip a card.

    Click or tap any face-down card to reveal its icon.

  2. Flip a second card.

    If the two icons match, both stay revealed. If not, they flip back after a moment.

  3. Clear the board.

    Keep finding pairs until every card is matched. Fewer moves and less time means a better score.
