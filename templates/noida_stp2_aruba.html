01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

Managing the Aruba network from deployment to operations.

The Infosys Noida STP-2 network infrastructure is built around HPE Aruba Networking technologies. I have been involved in managing the Aruba infrastructure from the beginning of the deployment and throughout its operational lifecycle.

The scope includes installation coordination, configuration, device management, routing, VLAN mapping, authentication, wireless infrastructure, BMS connectivity and software upgrades.

The Noida environment includes 25+ Layer 2 and Layer 3 devices managed with physical presence at the location, while the broader environment includes management of 500+ devices across locations.

03 · NETWORK OPERATIONS

Routing, VLANs and network mapping.

Day-to-day network operations include working with Aruba Layer 2 and Layer 3 infrastructure, routing and VLAN requirements across the Noida environment.

Activities include VLAN mapping, VLAN tagging, routing configuration and maintaining the required connectivity relationships between network infrastructure and connected systems.

Network administration is performed through Aruba Central and CLI-based access, with privileged access workflows supported through CyberArk.

05 · MANAGEMENT & SECURITY

Centralized management with secure administrative access.

Aruba infrastructure is managed using Aruba Central and direct CLI administration depending on the operational requirement.

Privileged device access is handled through CyberArk, providing a controlled workflow for administrative access to network infrastructure.

The environment also involves working with the HPE Aruba Networking GreenLake portal for management of the broader Aruba ecosystem.

06 · AOS-CX UPGRADE

Keeping the Aruba infrastructure up to date.

The Aruba infrastructure was previously operating on AOS-CX version 10.13.1010. As part of the infrastructure lifecycle and upgrade activities, the environment was upgraded to AOS-CX version 10.16.100.

The upgrade activity formed part of the ongoing management and maintenance of the Aruba network infrastructure.
