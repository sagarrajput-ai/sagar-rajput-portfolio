HOW TO PLAY

How to play Fruit Merge

  1. Aim and drop.

    Move your mouse or finger left and right, then click or tap to drop the current fruit into the jar.

  2. Merge matching fruit.

    When two fruits of the same type touch, they merge into the next, bigger fruit and add to your score.

  3. Keep the jar clear.

    If fruit stacks above the line near the top and stays there, the game ends.

  4. Chase the watermelon.

    The largest fruit is the watermelon. Merging two watermelons clears them both for a bonus.
