01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

Building the network foundation for a new enterprise site.

The Infosys Noida SEZ project involves the deployment and readiness of the network infrastructure required for a new enterprise site.

My involvement spans both technical infrastructure and operational coordination, covering network planning, data center setup, routing and switching, physical infrastructure readiness and coordination with vendors involved in the deployment.

The objective is to establish a reliable and properly prepared network environment that can support the site's operational requirements and transition into production readiness.

03 · INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING

Planning the infrastructure before deployment.

A new enterprise site requires coordination between network design, physical infrastructure and deployment activities. Planning therefore forms an important part of the project.

The work includes identifying infrastructure requirements, coordinating deployment dependencies and ensuring that the physical environment is prepared for network equipment and connectivity implementation.

Data center infrastructure, rack readiness, structured cabling and network equipment deployment are considered together rather than as isolated activities.

05 · VENDOR & SITE COORDINATION

Coordinating the physical and technical sides of deployment.

Enterprise infrastructure deployment depends on coordination between technical teams, site teams and external vendors.

I coordinate activities related to structured cabling, rack mounting and other physical infrastructure requirements needed for network deployment.

This also involves tracking dependencies, coordinating vendor activities and aligning physical infrastructure readiness with network implementation activities.

06 · END-TO-END SITE READINESS

Bringing the pieces together.

Network deployment is only one part of preparing a new enterprise site. Physical infrastructure, cabling, racks, network equipment, connectivity, configuration, testing and vendor activities all need to come together.

The focus of this project is therefore not simply configuring network devices, but helping ensure that the complete infrastructure environment is prepared for operational use.

07 · CHALLENGES & PROBLEM SOLVING

Infrastructure deployment is a coordination problem too.

New-site deployments involve multiple dependencies between physical infrastructure, network configuration, vendor activities and site availability.

A delay or issue in one area can affect activities across the deployment. This makes coordination, troubleshooting and continuous readiness tracking important throughout the project.

The project requires a combination of technical networking knowledge and an operational approach to identify issues, coordinate dependencies and keep infrastructure activities moving toward completion.
