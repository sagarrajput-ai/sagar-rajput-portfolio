How to play Cable Connect

  1. Rotate the tiles.

    Click or tap any cable tile to rotate it 90 degrees.

  2. Build the circuit.

    Connect cable ends together, starting from the Core Switch, to form an unbroken path.

  3. Light up every Endpoint.

    A path turns green when it's live. Reach every Endpoint to win - fewer rotations means a better score.
